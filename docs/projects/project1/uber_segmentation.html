Real World
Why segmentation matters
What is segmentation?
Divide customers into groups that are similar within each group and different between groups — so you can take different marketing actions for each.
A good segmentation is
Relevant
Substantial
Identifiable
Accessible
Actionable
"No one wants room-temperature tea. Don't make the average product — cater to the differences."
🎬
Netflix
Segments users by watch behavior — binge watchers, casual viewers, movie lovers — to personalize thumbnails and recommendations per profile.
↑ Reduces churn by keeping each segment engaged differently
🏰
Disney
Prices tickets by day and visitor type — SoCal residents, families, tourist peak days — using purchase data to maximize yield per segment.
↑ Same park, different price = segment-aware revenue management
🚗
Uber
Identifies rider archetypes from trip frequency, timing, and spend — then sends targeted promos: discounts to casuals, subscriptions to commuters.

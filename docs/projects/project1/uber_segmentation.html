Real World
Why segmentation matters
Every market has heterogeneous customers — different needs, different willingness to pay, different behavior patterns.
A single product or message aimed at the "average" customer ends up resonating with nobody — you leave value on the table.
Segmentation lets you allocate resources where they have the highest return — high-LTV segments get retention spend, price-sensitive segments get promotions.
